# Deal Discovery and Channel Optimization

Use with the Shopping Research Methodology for channel comparison, promo codes, cashback, rebate stacking or time-limited offers. A claimed discount is not savings until the conditions are verified.

## Standard mode
1. Establish exact item/variant and delivery destination; note any existing ownership or switching cost.
2. Check credible local retailers, official direct stores and relevant marketplaces, including delivery and pickup options.
3. Verify current listed price, shipping, taxes and stock; distinguish genuine equivalent variants from mismatches.
4. Examine straightforward, verifiable coupons and automatic discounts. Test code eligibility where authorized; don't report untested codes as valid.
5. Compute **dependable net cost** from confirmed payable amounts. Show conditional cashback, loyalty rewards, rebates or gift cards separately, with restrictions, expiry, tracking, and payout uncertainty.
6. Compare warranty, return window, seller support, dispute handling and fulfillment risk. Recommend based on net economics **and** reliability rather than headline discount.

## Deep mode — when the stakes justify it
Extend channel search to sale history, manufacturer promos, price matching, bundles, refurbished/open-box options, subscription thresholds, trade-ins and payment-channel offers. Model realistic stacking order and exclusions, caps, region restrictions, minimum spend, membership fees and opportunity costs. Do not treat uncertain sign-up incentives or unsupported coupon claims as cash. Check whether waiting makes sense and what risks price movement introduces.

## Output and boundaries
Show seller, exact variant, timestamp of checked offers, verified payable total, conditional savings and a clear caveat for uncertain promotions. Do not fabricate coupons or make purchases, enroll in memberships, or activate paid benefits without authorization.
